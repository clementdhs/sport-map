# Team-only access

- The app is first a personal one, shared with a team of family and friends: the texts say « équipe » / "team" since #130 (never « famille »), while code names (`family-only.ts`, `auth.login.familyOnly`, the e2e role `family`) keep the old word. Sign-ups are closed (`enable_signup = false` in `config.toml`, "Allow new users to sign up" off in the hosted dashboard) and accounts are created by hand in the dashboard, already confirmed. Their username starts as `user_…` (`handle_new_user`): give each member a real one (`clem`, `lea`) right away, in the settings or the `profiles` table, since it is also their sign-in name.
- Sign-in (`/login`): one field takes a username or an e-mail (`isEmailLogin`: an `@` means an e-mail). E-mails go straight to Supabase from the page. Usernames go to `POST /api/auth/login` (open endpoint), which finds the e-mail with `login_email` (service role only, never sent to the browser), signs in with a client of its own and returns only the tokens, which the page hands to `supabase.auth.setSession`. Unknown username and wrong password answer the same 401. Usernames are case-insensitive (unique index on `lower(username)`).
- Every username sign-in reaches Supabase from Vercel's IPs, so `consumeLoginAttempt` (`server/utils/loginAttempts.ts`, table `login_attempts`) limits attempts per typed username (5) and per caller IP (30, from `x-forwarded-for`, which Vercel sets) over 10 minutes; a success clears the username's count. Someone who knows a username can lock its username sign-in for 10 minutes: accepted, the e-mail still works.
- The account menu shows the username, not the e-mail (#87). It isn't in the session (dashboard accounts have no `user_metadata.username`, and it changes in the settings): `useMyProfile` fetches it once per member (SSR included), with the profile photo (`avatars.md`), and keeps it across pages; the settings page refreshes it after a change. Until then, or offline, the menu shows the e-mail. The e2e global setup empties the table, since every test signs in from the same IP.
- Pages: `auth.global.ts` sends anyone without a session to `/login?redirect=…`, except the sign-in pages listed in it. On the client it lets pages through when `navigator.onLine` is false, so a route prepared offline (follow mode) still opens when the session can't be refreshed.
- API: `server/middleware/family-only.ts` answers 401 to every `/api/` request without a session, reads included. New open endpoints go in its `OPEN_API` list. Policies only grant reads `to authenticated` ("public" means "visible to the team"); the anon role gets nothing.
- Two-factor authentication (#253), optional per member: `TwoFactorSettings` (`/settings#security`) enrols a TOTP app (`mfa.enroll` gives the QR code and the key; a first code confirms it with `mfa.challengeAndVerify`, which also turns the session into `aal2`), two apps at most (`max_enrolled_factors = 2` in `config.toml`), and removes one (`mfa.unenroll`, which Supabase only allows in an `aal2` session, then `refreshSession`). Unverified factors left by an abandoned enrolment are removed before the next one. Once a factor is verified, it is enforced everywhere: RLS through `public.mfa_ok()` (`aal2` session, or no verified factor) in `as restrictive` policies on every table and on `storage.objects` (`20261108200000_mfa_enforcement.sql`; a new table needs the same policy, `migrations.md`); routes that use the service role call `requireMfa(event)` (`server/utils/mfa.ts`: 403 `api.mfaRequired`), as does `consumeRoutingQuota` (its SQL function is security definer, and the quota protects OpenRouteService), and the security definer function `set_review_hidden` checks `mfa_ok()` itself; `og.png` stays open. Pages: `needsMfaCode()` (`app/utils/mfa.ts`: an `aal2` claim passes at once; otherwise the session's factors in the browser, `getUser()` on the server, where the cookie's user isn't trusted and reading it logs a warning) makes `auth.global.ts` send an `aal1` session of a protected account to `/login?redirect=…` (offline, it still lets pages through), where the page shows the « Code de vérification » step instead of the password form, after an e-mail or a username sign-in alike; the code is tried on each verified app, and « Changer de compte » signs out of this browser only (`scope: 'local'`: the default, global, would revoke the member's other sessions too, and anyone with the password could trigger it). The code is only asked at a new sign-in: refreshed sessions keep `aal2`.
- 2FA setup and recovery: the hosted project needs TOTP turned on by hand (dashboard, Authentication → Multi-Factor: "TOTP (App Authenticator)" enabled, 2 factors at most), as `config.toml` does locally (`npx supabase stop` / `start` after a change). A member who lost every app can't remove the factor themselves (no `aal2` session): delete it in the dashboard (Authentication → Users → the member → Multi-Factor Authentication, delete the factor); the member then signs in with their password alone and turns 2FA on again in the settings. No backup codes: a spare app (second factor) is the way out.
- Link previews: chat app bots have no session, so a shared route link lands on `/login`, which sets `og:image` to `/api/routes/:id/og.png` (open endpoint, service role, routes visible to the team only). `robots.txt` forbids everything but those two; pages say `noindex`.
- Sign-in page look (#110, #126, #237): `AuthScreen` is the backdrop of `/login`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password` and `/confirm`: the contour lines of the Écrins, drawn once by `scripts/login-background.py` from IGN RGE ALTI (Licence Ouverte 2.0: the credit at the bottom names the source and the data's revision date) into `public/img/login-contours.svg` (line art), used as an alpha mask filled with the signage's `--contour` tone (the hillshade under it went with the topo redesign), fading into the paper under the header, around the form and under the credit (kept small and pale in the corner, `text-muted` for AA), with a dotted track in the map's rust (`--map-track`) from an accent start dot (landscape and portrait SVGs). The form sits in `AuthCard` (surface, sand border, 18 px corners), its `UAuthForm` with `AUTH_FORM_UI` (title on the left in Barlow Condensed, no icon). Kept out of the service worker precache (`globIgnores`). Never name a `public/` folder like a page: the static server answers `/login` with a 301 to `/login/`, which the auth middleware then loops on. The hero's `h1` (logo, name, tagline) is bigger than the usual page title on purpose, and the page sets `headerLogo: false` in its meta so `AppHeader` drops its own logo (shown once). Texts visible to visitors name no team member (the username placeholder used to say `clem`).
- e2e: `specs/visitors.spec.ts` covers the redirects and 401s; the visitor flows run as another member (`test.use({ role: 'family' })`), who doesn't own the seeded routes. Requests as that member or without a session from a test signed in as the test account: `requestAs('family')` / `requestAs(null)` (`fixtures.ts`, `.claude/rules/e2e.md`).
