# Follow mode and outing recording

- Follow mode (`/routes/:id/follow`, no layout): `useWatchPosition` feeds `locateOnTrack` (`app/utils/trackFollower.ts`), which snaps each GPS fix to the track in a local flat frame and, where the track passes twice (loops, out-and-backs), keeps the passage closest to the previous progress. The screen is kept on with `useScreenWakeLock`. e2e tests drive it with Playwright's `geolocation` / `setGeolocation`.
- Outing recording (`/record`, no layout): pure functions in `app/utils/recorder.ts` over a serializable `Recording` (fix filtering, distance, moving time, pauses, GPS gaps), saved to `localStorage` so an outing survives a closed page: at most every `DRAFT_SAVE_MS` (15 s, #182: rewriting the whole outing after every point cost ~1.5 MB of JSON per second after 100 km), at once on start and pause, and when the page is hidden, hidden away (`pagehide`) or left. `POST /api/recordings` stores the track as a route (`source: 'recorded'`), a `completed` `route_activity` with `started_at`, `duration_s`, `moving_s`, and its first `route_outings` row (`saveRecording`, shared with the import of GPX, FIT and TCX outings: `outing-import.md`). Once saved, an outing counted in the coach's form opens the coach's review (`/coach/bilan/<outing id>`, `coach.md`), else the new route. A browser only records with the screen on; the Android app records with the screen off (`.claude/rules/android.md`). e2e tests drive it with `page.clock` and `setGeolocation`.
- GPS gaps (#180): a gap is GAP_MS without any fix, counted from `lastFixAt`, which fixes adding no point (standing still, paused) also refresh, at most every GAP_MS / 2 so a stop doesn't write the draft at every fix: a stop or a pause is not a gap. `/record` shows "GPS interrompu" for 6 s of visible page after each new gap, then nothing (Clem's choice: no lasting badge); a resumed draft's earlier gaps show nothing.
- Recording while following ("Enregistrer la sortie" in follow mode): same `useOutingRecorder` as `/record` (draft in `localStorage`, one key per route: `sport-map:recording:<id>`, leave guards), next to the in-memory `Recording` the guidance uses. `POST /api/routes/:id/outings` (`saveRouteOuting`, shared with the import of a watch outing on a route: `outing-import.md`) adds a `route_outings` row (times, GPS distance and GPS points; the route keeps its own track) and upserts the `completed` activity with this latest outing's times; no route is created. It answers the outing's id; once saved, an outing counted in the coach's form opens the coach's review (`coach.md`), else the route's Passages tab. `unique (user_id, route_id, started_at)` makes a save sent again a success, not a second outing. Saving offline fails and keeps the draft for later.
- Outings (`route_outings`): every GPS outing on a route, by anyone who can see it, listed in the route page's Passages tab (moving time against `estimateDurationMin`, gap rounded to 5 min). Profile statistics count each outing, plus once each route only marked as done without any outing (`statsOutings`), with the route's distance and gain.
- Outing detail (#57): both saves send `track`, the outing's GPS points as `[lng, lat, altitude | null, elapsed s, moving s so far]` (`outingTrack()`, thinned past `MAX_OUTING_POINTS`), stored in `route_outings.track` (`has_track` is generated, so lists don't load tracks). Each `RecordedPoint` keeps `movingMs`, the moving time when it was added: pauses and stops are left out of splits and speeds simply because it doesn't grow during them. Drafts recorded before have no `movingMs` and are saved without a track; outings saved before #57 have none either and show no "Détail". The Passages tab loads a track only when its detail opens (`GET /api/routes/:id/outings/:outingId`); `app/utils/splits.ts` (pure, unit-tested) gives splits per km (last stretch under 50 m left out, D+/D− from the GPS altitudes with the routes' smoothing, none without altitudes) and speed over 200 m of track around each of 200 samples. `OutingChart` plots pace linearly (s/km, faster up) for runners and walkers, km/h for cyclists (`SPORT_KINDS`), over the elevation in a neutral grey, bounds clipped to the 2nd–98th percentiles; hovering it moves the route page's map marker to the outing's own position. "Rejouer en 3D" replays it in a flyover (`flyover.md`).
- Data screen (`LiveData.vue`, "Données" button of both pages): big values over the map, which stays mounted (no MapLibre resize) but `covered`: like with the page hidden, `RouteFollow` then moves no camera and updates no layer, and catches up (track, arrow, camera) once the map shows again (#182). Values come from `app/utils/liveStats.ts`; follow mode also runs an in-memory `Recording` (`addFix`, never saved) for elapsed time, instant speed (last 30 s) and average moving speed, which gives the arrival time. "Remaining" values and the arrival only exist in follow mode (`MODE_FIELDS`). The chosen values per mode, in their chosen order (#181: checked ones go last, arrows move them; `toggleField` / `moveField`), and pace vs speed follow the member's account (`preferences.md`, cached in `sport-map:live-data`); pace is the default for running routes. Tiles size their numbers by both width and height (`container-type: size`), since many values make short tiles.
- Values added in #181: moving time (`movingMs`), max speed (`maxSpeedTracker`: the best 30 s window between recorded points, wall time so a move across a pause can't raise it; incremental, it only measures new points), D− and altitude (follow mode: the route's, from `TrackModel.lossLeft` / `elevations`, the GPS altitude when off track; recording: the GPS points' `elevationStats` and the latest fix), battery (`useBattery`, Battery Status API: Chromium and the Android WebView only; elsewhere it isn't offered and a choice made on another phone is hidden). Laps: the "Tour" button shows only with a lap value on screen and adds a `LapStart` to `Recording.laps` (`markLap`; kept in the draft, not sent to the server); follow mode laps on the recording when there is one, else on the in-memory outing, so starting a recording starts laps over.
- Voice guidance (follow mode only): `app/utils/voiceGuide.ts` decides what to announce from each fix (pure, unit-tested), `useVoiceGuide` turns it into sentences in the interface's language and filters by the choices kept in the account (`preferences.md`, cached in `sport-map:voice`, off by default, `useVoicePrefs`). The choices are set in follow mode's modal and on `/settings#voice`, through the same `VoiceGuideForm`. Every announcement is computed even when not chosen, so turning one on mid-outing doesn't bring back old news. Kilometres count the GPS distance actually covered (the in-memory `Recording`), not the progress along the route; climbs come from `climbs()` (`shared/utils/slopes.ts`) averaging at least 3 %, announced 300 m ahead. Leaving the route again within a minute of an announcement stays silent (GPS swinging around the limit).
- Speech (`app/utils/speech.ts`): `speechSynthesis` in browsers, which only speaks after a user action, so turning the guidance on speaks a confirmation. Android's WebView has no `speechSynthesis`: the app uses `@capacitor-community/text-to-speech`, which keeps speaking with the screen off since the GPS foreground service keeps the page running. e2e tests replace `speechSynthesis.speak` in an init script and read what was said.
- GPS arrow (`RouteFollow.vue`, both pages): the position is an arrow pointing the direction of travel, a dot while it's unknown, always with the accuracy halo. `app/utils/heading.ts` (pure, unit-tested) takes the device's course (`coords.heading` in browsers, `bearing` in the Android plugin) only at 0.5 m/s or more, otherwise measures it between fixes at least 10 m (or the accuracy) apart, ignoring fixes worse than 35 m and jumps. Stopped, the last arrow stays frozen and turns back into a dot after 2 min without a new direction: chosen before the field test, the thresholds may change after it. Since #98 the arrow shows `travelDirection()` (below): the phone compass replaces the frozen course when stopped.
- Map orientation (#98, `RouteFollow.vue`): a button in MapLibre's control group ("Orienter la carte dans le sens de la marche", `aria-pressed`) switches between north up (default) and heading up; the choice follows the account (`preferences.md`, cached in `sport-map:map-orientation`) and picking one also restarts following. `travelDirection()` (`app/utils/mapOrientation.ts`, pure, unit-tested) takes the GPS course while moving (measured within 10 s and, when the device gives a speed, at 1 m/s or more), else the compass, else the last course; a timer re-evaluates it once the course gets old, since a phone standing still may send no fix. A heading-up map aims at that direction smoothed by `smoothBearing` (63 % per second) and only turns once it is 5° away; all thresholds wait for the field test. MapLibre's easing already jumps under reduced motion (no `essential`). Gestures that pan or rotate (`originalEvent`) stop following, `v-model:follow` with the page's "Recentrer"; MapLibre's compass ("Réorienter vers le nord") switches to north up. Camera moves wait for the first zoom-in (`flyTo`) to end, which a fix arriving mid-flight used to stop half-way.
- Phone compass (`useCompass`, mounted with `RouteFollow`, paused while the page is hidden): `deviceorientationabsolute` in Chrome, Android and the Android app's WebView (no plugin; the hosted site is a secure context, a `CAP_SERVER_URL=http://…` debug build is not and gets no compass), else `deviceorientation` when `absolute` or with iOS's `webkitCompassHeading`. iOS needs `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission()` during a tap: the first tap on the page asks, so does turning heading up on. Recent Chromium (Playwright included) defines it too but sends the absolute event without it: never wait for it on that path. `screenHeading()` (`app/utils/compass.ts`) adds the top of the screen and the back of the phone laid flat (works flat, tilted or upright) and corrects for `screen.orientation.angle`; readings are smoothed (`smoothCompass`) and published at most every 100 ms in whole degrees. No reading within 3 s (computers) or a refusal: `available` false, and turning heading up says the map only turns while moving. e2e tests dispatch synthetic `DeviceOrientationEvent`s and read `data-map-bearing`.
- Live location sharing with the team ("Partager ma position", both pages, `useLiveShare` + `LiveShareControl`): `.claude/rules/live-sharing.md`.
- Battery (#182): `RouteFollow` pans with `jumpTo` (an `easeTo` redrew the map for 500 ms at every fix) and eases only to turn or during an easing; a recorded track is redrawn at most every 3 s (MapLibre tiles the whole line again each time), the GPS arrow showing the latest position. The native GPS keeps `distanceFilter: 0`: a filter saves little and would stop fixes when standing still, counted as a gap. Before/after battery measures are Clem's field test.

# Topo look of follow mode and recording (#235)

From the canvas's "Suivre un parcours" and "Enregistrer — données" boards (#226).

- Follow mode: a `map-float` top bar (quit link "Quitter" as an icon, "Suivi en cours" over the route's name, outdoor readability and voice guidance), then `ArrivalSign` (yellow sign "Arrivée · remaining distance · duration", `data-testid="remaining"`; the duration is `remainingDurationMin`: the average moving speed once known, else the route estimate with the member's pace over what is left), hidden on the data screen and once arrived. Alerts are solid (sun). The bottom panel is on the paper with 22 px top corners: `FollowProfile` (mini profile, covered part in `--map-track-done`, GPS-blue position; `UProgress` without elevations), three ruled figures (Parcouru, Temps, Allure or Vitesse as on the data screen, `live-label` / `live-figure` / `live-unit` utilities of `main.css`), then "Données / Carte" and "Enregistrer la sortie" (or the recording's status, Pause and Terminer, 56 px). The panel doesn't overlap the map: its scale and attribution stay visible.
- The covered part of the route on the map is a `line-gradient` (`doneGradient`, `setRouteDone` in `routeLayers.ts`, colour `APP_MAP_COLORS.trackDone`: darker in light mode, lighter in dark mode), redrawn only past a thousandth of the route and never while the map is covered or hidden (`RouteFollow` `doneFraction`).
- `/record`: the top bar holds the status as an ink pill with the stopwatch (`data-testid="elapsed"`, words only from sm up), the panel three figures (distance, speed, D+) and Pause / Terminer.
- Data screen: the first chosen value very large on the paper (sized by its box, `container-type: size`), the route's sport and name under it in follow mode, the others in a two-column `KeyFigures` cartouche (`columns`, `fill`: rows share the height, figures sized by their cell), each cell keeping `data-field`. Recording's default values start with the distance, then the mockup's six (moving time, average speed, D+, D−, altitude, max speed); its elapsed time is in the top bar.
