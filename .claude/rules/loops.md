# Automatic loops (#50)

- `POST /api/routing/loop` (signed-in) gives one proposal: OpenRouteService directions with `options.round_trip` (`length`, `points: 3`, `seed`), 100 km at most (ORS limit). ORS only aims at the length: detours made test loops up to 2.5 times too long around Grenoble, so the endpoint asks again with `nextLoopLength` (scaled by the error, then the secant through the last two tries) until ORS's `summary.distance` is within 10 % (`isOnTarget`), at most `LOOP_ATTEMPTS` = 3 calls, each counted by `consumeRoutingQuota`. Off target after that: 422 `api.loopOffTarget`. On test starts, 8 directions out of 10 converged in the Alps, all in flat country. `points: 5` gave rounder loops but further from the length.
- Elevations (#176): only the accepted loop is sent to the IGN (Open-Meteo abroad) by `withMeasuredElevations`, after the tries; rejected tries never call it, and it doesn't count in the quota. When those services fail or are slow, the loop keeps ORS's elevations, whose 0 m are filled first (`withoutZeroElevations`). See "Routed elevations" in `routes-data.md`.
- `useLoopGeneratorStore` sends one request per proposal (`LOOP_PROPOSALS` = 3, consecutive seeds from a random one), in parallel, each row showing its own error; a quota refusal goes through `editor.isRoutingQuotaError` (warns once, like segments). A duration becomes a distance at the member's pace, else the sport's default, climbing the chosen climbing's `typicalGainPerKm` (`loopLengthForDuration`, `estimateDurationMin`, `paces.md`; #214). Changing the start, the sport, the shape or the place to go through clears the proposals (watched with `flush: 'sync'`, so a change made just before `generate()` doesn't clear the new ones); answers for cleared proposals are ignored (`generation`).
- One "Créer" panel (#215): `RouteEditorWorkspace` opens with a mode bar (`UTabs`, "Boucle" / "À la main"; `loop.open` is the mode). The create page (`remember-mode`) opens in the mode of `?mode=loop|manual`, else the last one picked on the device (`app/utils/createMode.ts`, `sport-map:create-mode`, read on mount, ignored when a draft has a track, e.g. one imported from the marked routes), and writes each change back with `router.replace`; the edit page always opens by hand. The start is shared: `show()` takes the track's first point when there is one, `switchToManual()` makes the loop's start the first waypoint of an empty track, `leave()` (unmount) forgets it so a saved route doesn't lend its start to the next. "Clique sur la carte pour placer le départ." is said once, in the layout's `overlay` slot (`role="status"`, with "Annuler" when a loop start already exists); "Importer un fichier GPX" sits at the bottom of the panel in both modes, and an import switches to drawing by hand.
- While the panel is open, `RouteEditor.vue` hides the editor's track and its clicks only place the start (`placingStart`) or highlight a proposal; dragging waypoints is off. Proposal colours (`LOOP_COLORS`) are the dataviz categorical slots 1–3, light steps on the light map and dark steps on the dark one (#234: the mockup's blue and violet failed the validator, ΔE 3.1 under deutan), validated all-pairs on each map background; the light steps sit under 3:1 on the paper, so the list names every loop and the lines keep their casing. Not in `APP_MAP_COLORS`: `switchAppColors` would also swap the way info's and marked routes' identical literals; `addLoopLayers` repaints them on each `style.load` instead. The highlighted loop is drawn last by rebuilding the source (three lines), since a feature state can't change the drawing order.
- Choosing a loop: `editor.loadLoop` cuts it at `loopWaypointIndices` (about every 2 km, 4 to 10 segments) into `snap` segments that keep their slice of elevations and way info: no new routing, and moving a waypoint routes only its two segments. Undo brings back the previous track.
- Repeated "Autres propositions" (up to 9 ORS calls each) can hit ORS's per-minute limit: rows then show `api.routingTooFast`.
- e2e: `fixtures.ts` mocks `/api/routing/loop` with a square of the asked length, turned by the seed.

# Loop options (#214)

- **Climbing** "Plat" / "Vallonné" / "Costaud" (`LOOP_CLIMBS`): no routing option, ORS can't aim at a gain. The 3 proposals are ranked once every answer is in (`rankByClimb`: flattest first, hilliest first, or closest to the middle), and again for free when the climbing changes, the highlighted one staying highlighted; each ready row shows its own climbing (`climbOf`). Thresholds per sport in `CLIMB_THRESHOLDS` (gain per km, flat up to / hilly from: running 8 / 20, trail and hiking 15 / 40, road cycling 5 / 12…). `rankByGain(proposals, perKm)` ranks closest first to any gain per km: a planned session's D+ over its distance (#266) goes through it.
- **"Longueur approximative (± 20 %)"** (`precision: 'range'`): `LOOP_TOLERANCES.range` = 0.2 instead of 0.1 in `isOnTarget`, so fewer tries refused and more varied loops.
- **Out and back** (`shape: 'out_and_back'`): ORS routes the way out only, to a far end at half the length / `PATH_DETOUR` (1.3) as the crow flies, bearing `seedBearing(seed)` (golden angle, so the 3 seeds point apart); its length counts double; the accepted one is measured once by `withMeasuredElevations`, then `outAndBack` appends the same points reversed and mirrors the way info ranges. Off target: `api.outAndBackOffTarget`. Proposals are then named "Aller-retour n".
- **Through a place** (`via`): the browser asks Overpass around the start (`searchLoopVia`, `loopViaQuery`: peaks with an `ele`, drinking water as in `osmPlaceType`) within `viaSearchRadius` (going there and back fits in the length, 10 km at most), keeps the answer for "Autres propositions", and `pickLoopVia` takes the highest peak, or the water point nearest to halfway. None in reach or Overpass down: a `role="status"` line says so and the proposals go without it. The server then routes `loopRoutePoints` instead of a round trip: a loop start → place → a third corner (`loopCorner`, side and opening from the seed) → start; an out and back start → place → a far end beyond it (or just the place when it is already far enough). Snapping radiuses: 350 m start, 1 km place (peaks are often off the paths), -1 for the app's points. A loop already as short as the place allows goes straight there and back (start → place → start). A `via` further than `VIA_SEARCH_MAX_M` from the start is refused before any ORS call (`isViaInReach`). When the shortest way through the place is already too long, asking again can't help: 422 `api.loopViaTooFar` at once (`isShortestThrough`); no path to it: `api.loopViaNotFound`. The place is drawn as a ringed point (`loop-via`) in the start's source.
- Quota: unchanged, at most 3 proposals × `LOOP_ATTEMPTS` ORS calls per generation; Overpass is free (2 queries at a time per IP, `overpass.ts`).
- e2e: `editor.spec.ts` mocks `**/api/interpreter` with one peak near the start.

# Topo look of the Créer panel (#234)

From the canvas's "Créer — boucle automatique" boards. The mode bar is a segmented control (`UTabs` restyled: `bg-elevated` track, current mode on the surface). Both modes pick the sport with `SportChips` (`useSportChips`, short names). Loop panel: distance or duration as small-capitals tabs with the figure written beside, set with a `USlider` (1–100 km, 10–600 min by 5), then the "± 20 %" and "Aller-retour" switches (a switch, not tabs: the mode bar already has a "Boucle" tab, and two tabs of one name broke the e2e locators), the climbing tabs and the "Passer par" select (#214); the start as the map's yellow square with "Ma position" (icon) and "Déplacer"; proposals as `aria-pressed` buttons (colour stroke, figures, `DurationSign`): the picked one is the highlighted one, else the first ready once every answer is in (`generating` over), synced to `loop.highlighted` for the map; "Autres propositions" (ink outline) and "Choisir cette boucle", the quota hint under them. The map's instruction is on the ink (`bg-inverted`), "…déplacer le départ" with "Annuler" when a start exists. By hand: the snap switch beside undo / clear / help, the distance then D+ / D− / duration in `KeyFigures` (duration on the yellow, pace tooltip), secondary buttons outlined in ink. No place name for the start: no reverse geocoding (MapTiler quota).
