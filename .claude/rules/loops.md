# Automatic loops (#50)

- `POST /api/routing/loop` (signed-in) gives one proposal: OpenRouteService directions with `options.round_trip` (`length`, `points: 3`, `seed`), 100 km at most (ORS limit). ORS only aims at the length: detours made test loops up to 2.5 times too long around Grenoble, so the endpoint asks again with `nextLoopLength` (scaled by the error, then the secant through the last two tries) until ORS's `summary.distance` is within 10 % (`isOnTarget`), at most `LOOP_ATTEMPTS` = 3 calls, each counted by `consumeRoutingQuota`. Off target after that: 422 `api.loopOffTarget`. On test starts, 8 directions out of 10 converged in the Alps, all in flat country. `points: 5` gave rounder loops but further from the length.
- Elevations (#176): only the accepted loop is sent to the IGN (Open-Meteo abroad) by `withMeasuredElevations`, after the tries; rejected tries never call it, and it doesn't count in the quota. When those services fail or are slow, the loop keeps ORS's elevations, whose 0 m are filled first (`withoutZeroElevations`). See "Routed elevations" in `routes-data.md`.
- `useLoopGeneratorStore` sends one request per proposal (`LOOP_PROPOSALS` = 3, consecutive seeds from a random one), in parallel, each row showing its own error; a quota refusal goes through `editor.isRoutingQuotaError` (warns once, like segments). A duration becomes a distance at the member's pace, else the sport's default, climbing the chosen climbing's `typicalGainPerKm` (`loopLengthForDuration`, `estimateDurationMin`, `paces.md`; #214). Changing the start, the sport, the shape or the place to go through clears the proposals (watched with `flush: 'sync'`, so a change made just before `generate()` doesn't clear the new ones); answers for cleared proposals are ignored (`generation`).
- One "Créer" panel (#215): `RouteEditorWorkspace` opens with a mode bar (`UTabs`, "Boucle" / "À la main"; `loop.open` is the mode). The create page (`remember-mode`) opens in the mode of `?mode=loop|manual`, else the last one picked on the device (`app/utils/createMode.ts`, `sport-map:create-mode`, read on mount, ignored when a draft has a track, e.g. one imported from the marked routes), and writes each change back with `router.replace`; the edit page always opens by hand. The start is shared: `show()` takes the track's first point when there is one, `switchToManual()` makes the loop's start the first waypoint of an empty track, `leave()` (unmount) forgets it so a saved route doesn't lend its start to the next. "Clique sur la carte pour placer le départ." is said once, in the layout's `overlay` slot (`role="status"`, with "Annuler" when a loop start already exists); "Importer un fichier GPX" sits at the bottom of the panel in both modes, and an import switches to drawing by hand.
- While the panel is open, `RouteEditor.vue` hides the editor's track and its clicks only place the start (`placingStart`) or highlight a proposal; dragging waypoints is off. Proposal colours (`LOOP_COLORS`) are the dataviz categorical slots 1–3, light steps on the light map and dark steps on the dark one (#234: the mockup's blue and violet failed the validator, ΔE 3.1 under deutan), validated all-pairs on each map background; the light steps sit under 3:1 on the paper, so the list names every loop and the lines keep their casing. Not in `APP_MAP_COLORS`: `switchAppColors` would also swap the way info's and marked routes' identical literals; `addLoopLayers` repaints them on each `style.load` instead. The highlighted loop is drawn last by rebuilding the source (three lines), since a feature state can't change the drawing order.
- Choosing a loop: `editor.loadLoop` cuts it at `loopWaypointIndices` (about every 2 km, 4 to 10 segments) into `snap` segments that keep their slice of elevations and way info: no new routing, and moving a waypoint routes only its two segments. Undo brings back the previous track.
- Repeated "Autres propositions" (up to 9 ORS calls each) can hit ORS's per-minute limit: rows then show `api.routingTooFast`.
- e2e: `fixtures.ts` mocks `/api/routing/loop` with a square of the asked length, turned by the seed.

# Loop options (#214)

- **Climbing** "Plat" / "Vallonné" / "Costaud" (`LOOP_CLIMBS`): no routing option, ORS can't aim at a gain. The 3 proposals are ranked once every answer is in (`rankByClimb`: flattest first, hilliest first, or closest to the middle), and again for free when the climbing changes, the highlighted one staying highlighted; each ready row shows its own climbing (`climbOf`). Thresholds per sport in `CLIMB_THRESHOLDS` (gain per km, flat up to / hilly from: running 8 / 20, trail and hiking 15 / 40, road cycling 5 / 12…). `rankByGain(proposals, perKm)` ranks closest first to any gain per km: a planned session's D+ over its distance goes through it (`rankForSession`, #266).
- **A gain in metres** (#294, the "D+" target tab, `target: 'gain'`, `gainM`): ORS can't aim at a gain either. Without a distance, the length is the one a hilly outing of the sport needs to climb it (`loopLengthForGain`: gain / `typicalGainPerKm(sport, 'hilly')`, 600 m on a bike = 32 km, within 1–100 km); "Fixer aussi la distance" (`gainWithDistance`) keeps the distance slider's length instead. The slider runs 100 m to `loopGainRange(sport).max` (what a hilly 100 km climbs, 3000 m at most: 1850 on a bike, 1500 on a road bike, 2500 on foot), `gainTargetM` clamping a gain kept from another sport. The proposals are ranked by `rankByTotalGain` (closest in metres first), again for free when the gain or the target changes; the climbing tabs and badges are hidden. When no ready proposal comes within `GAIN_TOLERANCE` (25 %), `gainShortfall` says whether the terrain is too flat or climbs too much, with the closest gain (`loop.gainMissed`, a `role="status"` line): said, not forced. A duration with a gain isn't supported.
- **"Longueur approximative (± 20 %)"** (`precision: 'range'`): `LOOP_TOLERANCES.range` = 0.2 instead of 0.1 in `isOnTarget`, so fewer tries refused and more varied loops.
- **Out and back** (`shape: 'out_and_back'`): ORS routes the way out only, to a far end at half the length / `PATH_DETOUR` (1.3) as the crow flies, bearing `seedBearing(seed)` (golden angle, so the 3 seeds point apart); its length counts double; the accepted one is measured once by `withMeasuredElevations`, then `outAndBack` appends the same points reversed and mirrors the way info ranges. Off target: `api.outAndBackOffTarget`. Proposals are then named "Aller-retour n".
- **Through a place** (`via`): the browser asks Overpass around the start (`searchLoopVia`, `loopViaQuery`: peaks with an `ele`, drinking water as in `osmPlaceType`) within `viaSearchRadius` (going there and back fits in the length, 10 km at most), keeps the answer for "Autres propositions", and `pickLoopVia` takes the highest peak, or the water point nearest to halfway. None in reach or Overpass down: a `role="status"` line says so and the proposals go without it. The server then routes `loopRoutePoints` instead of a round trip: a loop start → place → a third corner (`loopCorner`, side and opening from the seed) → start; an out and back start → place → a far end beyond it (or just the place when it is already far enough). Snapping radiuses: 350 m start, 1 km place (peaks are often off the paths), -1 for the app's points. A loop already as short as the place allows goes straight there and back (start → place → start). A `via` further than `namedPlaceReach` (#326) from the start, or a tour through the `vias` longer than `maxTourCrowM`, is refused before any ORS call (`isViaInReach`, `tourCrowM`: 422 `api.loopViaTooFar`). When the shortest way through the place is already too long, asking again can't help: 422 `api.loopViaTooFar` at once (`isShortestThrough`); no path to it: `api.loopViaNotFound`. The place is drawn as a ringed point (`loop-via`) in the start's source.
- **A place the member names** (#293, `via: 'named'`, "Un lieu que je nomme" + a textbox, `viaName`): `searchNamedPlaces` asks Overpass for `namedPlaceQuery` in a box `NAMED_PLACE_SEARCH_M` (30 km) on each side of the start (`around` that far with a name regex was cut by the 25 s timeout on a busy instance; even the box takes 40–60 s when Overpass is loaded), only rivers / lakes (ways with `out geom`), peaks, mountain ranges, `place=region|city|town|village|hamlet` and protected areas (`out center`), never administrative boundaries (« Isère » would be the département). The name is matched on its core (`namedPlaceCore`: no accents, case, articles or generic words such as lac, massif, chaîne, rivière, parc naturel régional), the regex listing accented capitals since Overpass' `,i` only folds ASCII. `pickNamedPlace`: exact core matches first (else those containing it, « Sappey-en-Chartreuse »), the kind said with the name when there are some (« lac Chartreuse »), then massif > river > lake > peak > village, then the nearest (a river's nearest point on its line, else the centre). Around Grenoble « Chartreuse » finds the `place=region` relation « Massif de la Chartreuse » (22 km), the PNR, a « Lac Chartreuse » and seven villages. A non-massif within `namedPlaceReach` (#326; `viaSearchRadius` before) is a `via` (the usual route through it); a massif or anything further only gives `heading` (its bearing, sent instead of `via`): the server's `loopRoutePoints` then routes a loop through a point crowM / 3 that way and a third corner, or an out and back to crowM / 2 that way ± `VIA_SPREAD`, both snapped anywhere (-1). Nothing found: `viaStatus` 'none', the proposals go without it. The map's ringed points are `loop.viaPoints` (none for a heading).
- **Sources of a named place** (#310, `shared/utils/namedPlaces.ts`, `app/utils/namedPlaces.ts`): Overpass alone was too fragile (all instances down on 2026-11-11). `namedPlaceSources` picks from the generic word said with the name: « village » / « ville » → MapTiler's geocoding only (`types` = settlements, `bbox` = `namedPlaceBox`, `proximity` = the start, 5 answers, one request per search: quota); « lac », « mont », « rivière », « massif »… → OpenStreetMap only; a bare name (« Seyssinet », « Chartreuse ») → both, in parallel, merged. OpenStreetMap is Overpass, and Nominatim (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`, `bounded` to the box, `polygon_geojson` for a river's line) only when Overpass failed, only when Overpass failed: the name as said, then its core when that found nothing (`nominatimRetryName`: it finds « l'Isère » but only the département for « isere », and « moucherotte » but nothing for « le mont Moucherotte »), so two requests at most, spaced 1 s in the tab (usage policy; the browser's Referer identifies the app, no bulk, never from tests: `fixtures.ts` mocks it). Nominatim gives a commune as its `boundary=administrative` with `addresstype` village/town/city (kept as a village, a département never). `searchNamedPlacesWith` throws only when no source answered (`viaStatus` 'failed'); otherwise `pickNamedPlace` chooses among everything, its role rules unchanged. MapTiler's `ref` `osm:r191039` becomes Overpass' id `r191039`. A bare name still waits for Overpass (up to its 60 s budget) before the choice, so a massif of that name isn't lost to a village MapTiler found; the cache below spares it for a known start.
- Quota: unchanged, at most 3 proposals × `LOOP_ATTEMPTS` ORS calls per generation; Overpass is free (2 queries at a time per IP, `overpass.ts`).
- e2e: `editor.spec.ts` mocks `**/api/interpreter` with one peak near the start, and with a river / a massif by the query's name pattern (#293); with Overpass answering 504, a commune from MapTiler and a peak from Nominatim (#310).

# Preferring paths (#295)

- OpenRouteService has no option to avoid tarmac (`avoid_features` only knows highways, tollways, ferries, fords, steps; surface restrictions are the wheelchair profile's). « Préférer les chemins » (`preferPaths`) therefore routes the loops on a profile that takes more paths, `loopProfile`: runs on `foot-hiking` (instead of `foot-walking`), cycling on `cycling-mountain` (instead of `cycling-regular`); hiking, trail and mountain biking already are. Same number of ORS calls. Road cycling isn't offered the option (`canPreferPaths`; the switch is hidden, `pathsActive` false, no `prefer_paths` sent).
- Tried on 10 km foot / 25 km bike round trips on 2026-11-11: Meylan foot 1→6 % and 14→27 % of path, bike 21→71 % and 9→36 %; Eupen bike 12→24 % and 20→51 %; Lille 0 % whatever the profile. The `green` weighting (foot profiles) was dropped: no effect at Meylan, and loops 1.5 to 2.5 times too long at Eupen (more tries, more quota).
- The proposals carry `pathShare` (`pathShare` in `wayInfo.ts`: unpaved and trail classes of `surfaceClass` over the whole length, unknown ways counting as not paths; null without way info) and are ranked by `rankByPaths` after `rankByClimb`, by tenths so that close shares keep the climbing's order. Each row shows « n % de chemin ». When every proposal with known ways is under `FEW_PATHS_SHARE` (10 %), a status line says the loops stay on roads (in town, nothing to pick from).
- A sentence's paths are left aside when the editor's sport is road cycling and the sentence names none (no chip, no setting). Choosing a paths loop keeps its points, but its segments are filed under the sport's own profile (`loadLoop`, `currentProfile`): moving a waypoint re-routes its two segments on `foot-walking` / `cycling-regular`, not the paths profile (filing them under the paths profile would raise the editor's "another sport" warning at once).
- Toggling the option clears the proposals when it changes the profile (running, cycling), else ranks them again for free. The session mode ignores it (the coach's road run).

# Session mode (#266)

- A third mode of the panel on the create page, « Séance » (`?mode=session`, `loop.showSession()`): the store's `forSession` with the coach's `session` plan (`setSession`, set by `SessionRoute.vue`; the coach's rules in `coach.md`). `targetM` is the session's distance, the requests plain loops (`loop`, `precise`, no `via`) whatever the loop mode's options, the sport `running`; each ready proposal carries its `climbs` (`sessionClimbs`, hill repeats only) and the proposals are ranked by `rankForSession` instead of `rankByClimb`. # Loop described in a sentence (#216)

- "Décris ta sortie" at the top of the loop mode (`LoopSentence.vue`, not in the session mode, whose distance is the coach's): Enter (or the arrow) sends the sentence alone to `POST /api/loops/understand` (signed in, `consumeLoopSentenceQuota`: `consume_loop_sentence_quota`, `loop_sentence_usage`, `NUXT_LOOP_SENTENCE_DAILY_LIMIT` 30 a day, 429 with `LOOP_SENTENCE_QUOTA_REASON`; counted before the LLM call). No key (`NUXT_LOOP_LLM_KEY`): 503 `api.loopSentenceUnavailable`. Groq's free tier is 8,000 tokens a minute for the whole organization (a sentence asks ~1,500): a 429 is waited out up to `LOOP_LLM_MAX_WAIT_MS` (10 s), then 503 `api.loopSentenceBusy` « réessaie dans une minute » (`loopLlmFailure`, #304). The quota is shared by every key of the organization: the reference series run with the production key starved the site (Sentry JAVASCRIPT-NUXT-6), so development should use a key from another Groq organization. Other LLM failures: 502; answer invalid or nothing usable: 422 `api.loopSentenceNotUnderstood`, shown as "Je n'ai pas compris ta phrase." with the manual settings untouched. No fallback parser (the issue's choice).
- Provider-independent: prompt (`LOOP_SENTENCE_PROMPT`), strict JSON schema (`LOOP_SENTENCE_JSON_SCHEMA`: every property required, `additionalProperties: false`, nullable as type unions or `anyOf` with null) and conversion (`loopSettingsFromUnderstanding`) in `shared/utils/loopSentence.ts`; `server/utils/loopLlm.ts` has one adapter per `NUXT_LOOP_LLM_PROVIDER` (`groq`, default, `openai/gpt-oss-120b` with `reasoning_effort: 'low'` and `include_reasoning: false`; `openai`, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint through `NUXT_LOOP_LLM_URL`). The answer is checked again by `loopUnderstandingSchema` (unknown fields dropped). The LLM never gives coordinates, only names.
- The sentence fills only what it mentions (`loop.applySentence`, which returns the settings it changed, marked in yellow until the next sentence; chips under "Compris" scroll to their setting): sport (the editor's), distance (rounded to the km, 1–100) or duration (5 min, 10–600; out of range: reported, not filled), "environ" → ± 20 % (only with a target), shape, climbing, and the named places (#293, #326: `places`, which set « Passer par » to them, one field each; their roles are OpenStreetMap's, never the LLM's; beyond `LOOP_MAX_VIAS` they are quoted back). Paths (#295, `paths: true`) turn « Préférer les chemins » on, except when the sentence's sport is road cycling (quoted back, `ignored` kind `paths`). A gain in metres (#294: `gain`, rounded to 50 m, 100–3000) switches to the "D+" target, with the sentence's distance kept (`gainWithDistance`) or alone, and replaces the climbing level; with a duration, or out of range, it is quoted back (`ignored`, kind `gain`).
- Start: "mon point" (or none said) = the start already placed, else the device's position (`useCurrentPosition`'s `onFailed`); a named start is geocoded with MapTiler (`limit: 1`, `proximity` = the map's centre), only then. Not found or no position: "Départ : à placer" and a box with "Ma position" / "Placer sur la carte"; the loops follow once the start is placed. Then `generate()` when `canGenerate`.
- Reference sentences: `test/llm/loopSentences.ts` (about 30, FR and EN, expected settings), run against the real LLM by hand with `npm run test:loop-sentences` (`vitest.llm.config.ts`, reads `NUXT_LOOP_LLM_*` from `.env`, skipped without a key, never in CI) after changing the prompt, schema, model or provider.
- e2e: `editor.spec.ts` mocks `**/api/loops/understand` in the test.

# Cache of the loop panel's searches (#311)

- `loop_place_searches` (key → `places` jsonb, `fetched_at`; select to members, written only by `POST /api/loops/places` with the service role after `requireMfa` and the zod check `loopPlacesSaveSchema`; read by `POST /api/loops/places/lookup` as the member) keeps the peaks / water points (`OsmPlace[]`) and the places of a name (`NamedPlace[]`) per cell of a 0.02° grid (`shared/utils/loopPlacesCache.ts`). Key: `peak` / `water`, or `named` + language + the sources the name asks (`namedPlaceSources`: « village Chartreuse » isn't « Chartreuse ») + `namedPlaceCore`, then the cell's indices (`loopPlacesCacheKey`).
- Searches are made around the **cell centre** (`loopPlacesCell`), never the exact start, so every start of the cell shares them: peaks and water within `LOOP_VIA_CACHE_RADIUS_M` (`VIA_SEARCH_MAX_M` + 1.6 km, whatever the loop's length; `pickLoopVia` keeps those within the loop's radius from the real start), named places in `namedPlaceBox(centre)`, then measured again from the start (`namedPlacesFromCell`). A river's point is its nearest to the centre, up to 1.6 km off the start's nearest.
- `cachedLoopPlaces` (`app/utils/loopPlacesCache.ts`): an answer under `LOOP_PLACES_TTL_DAYS` (30) serves without any request to Overpass / MapTiler / Nominatim; an older one is asked again, and still serves when the live search fails. A lookup failure only means asking live. Kept only when complete: for a name, every source `namedPlaceSources` asks answered (MapTiler, and Overpass or Nominatim in its place), so a massif missed while OSM was down isn't missed for a month (an incomplete live answer gives way to an older complete one); peaks and water keep the `MAX_LOOP_CACHED_PLACES` (2000) nearest to the centre (`nearestLoopPlaces`). Empty answers are kept too.
- e2e: `editor.spec.ts` empties the table before each test (every test starts at the map's centre, so the same cell); its #311 test checks Overpass is asked once, then the kept peak serves with Overpass answering 504.
# Several named places, further than 10 km (#326)

- **Reach.** A named place is gone through when going there and back fits the length: `namedPlaceReach` = `maxTourCrowM(length, tolerance) / 2` (`length × (1 + tolerance) / PATH_DETOUR / 2`), capped at `NAMED_PLACE_SEARCH_M` (30 km): from Grenoble, Autrans (14.3 km) for a 70 km loop (29.6 km). `VIA_SEARCH_MAX_M` (10 km) only bounds the peak and water search (`viaSearchRadius`, the Overpass `around`); the server's `isViaInReach` uses `namedPlaceReach` for every via (a peak or water point is always within it). A massif stays a heading.
- **Several names.** « Passer par » → « Un lieu que je nomme » shows one field per name (`viaNames`, « Ajouter un lieu », a remove button once there are two), `LOOP_MAX_VIAS` (5, in `routes.ts` to avoid a circular import) at most. Each name is searched as before (#293, #310, the #311 cache per name), in parallel (`findAllNamed`); each gets its own result (`namedResults`: found / none / failed / vague) and its own `role="status"` line.
- **Plan.** `namedPlacesPlan`: the places to go through go through `fitVias`, which drops those out of reach, those beyond `LOOP_MAX_VIAS`, then, while the tour (`tourCrowM`, as the crow flies) is longer than `maxTourCrowM`, the one whose detour costs the most (`prev → place → next` minus `prev → next`); with no place to go through, the first heading is used; the rest is `setAside` and said (« ne tient pas dans cette longueur », or « donne seulement une direction » beside places to go through). The plan is made once per generation, with the generation's length and shape.
- **Order.** `orderVias`: a loop takes its places by bearing around the start, from the one after the widest gap between bearings (so Lans-en-Vercors at 238° then Autrans at 264°, never back across the start's side), clockwise for even seeds and counter-clockwise for odd ones; an out and back takes them nearest first. The server orders the `vias` itself.
- **Routing.** `/api/routing/loop` takes `vias` (array, `LOOP_MAX_VIAS` at most; the old single `via` is still read, for an app still cached). With one place nothing changes (third corner). With several (`routeThroughPlaces`), a loop goes start → places → start, snapped at 1 km each, with no random third corner; when that tour is shorter than the loop (crowM > tour), one leg, picked by `seed % legs` (so the 3 consecutive seeds take 3 different legs, and alternate the direction), gets a detour corner (`detourCorner`, on the perpendicular bisector, on the side away from the stops' centre, -1 snapping) making the tour as long as asked; the tries then reshape it like a third corner. When the tour is already the shortest (`isShortestThrough` with every via), a loop too long is `api.loopViaTooFar` at once. Quota unchanged.
- **Out and back with several places** (decided in #326): the way out goes through them nearest first, then beyond the last one, along the start → last bearing ± `VIA_SPREAD`, for the rest of half the length; or stops at the last when there is no room (`MIN_LEG_M`); the way back is the same reversed (`outAndBack`). The tour measured for the fit is twice the way out.
- e2e: `editor.spec.ts` fills three villages from a sentence (MapTiler mocked, Overpass empty): two west go through, the one 26 km south-east is set aside, the requests send both in bearing order.
