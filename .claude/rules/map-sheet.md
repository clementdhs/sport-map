# Main navigation, map pages: side panel and mobile bottom sheet

- Main navigation (#101, `useMainNav`): Carte (`/`, also current on route pages), Découvrir, En direct (`/live`), Profil (also current on collections), with `aria-current` (`page` on the section itself, `true` on its sub-pages). Below md, `AppTabBar` at the bottom (labels always shown, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) with a central "Créer ou enregistrer" button opening a menu (Enregistrer une sortie, Créer un parcours); from md up, `AppHeader` holds the same sections and the two buttons, on every page but the full-screen ones. Full screen, without header nor tab bar: editor (`MapSheetLayout fullscreen`), follow, record, a live share (`/live/:id`), which keep their own "Quitter" or back button. `AccountMenu` keeps only the account: settings, language, sign out.
- Home, discover, route page, editor and collection page use `MapSheetLayout` (slots `map`, `title`, `search`, `overlay`, default) and `definePageMeta({ layout: false })`. The layout is a `h-dvh` column: `AppHeader` (md up), its root (map, sheet, panel), `AppTabBar` (phones), so every measure taken on the root (sheet positions, `useMapPadding`) already leaves the bars out. Other pages use the default layout: `AppHeader` (no border; below md only logo and account), and the tab bar fixed at the bottom with room kept under `UMain`. From md up the panel sits beside the map; below md the map fills the screen under a bottom sheet with three positions (`data-sheet` = `collapsed` / `half` / `full`, which stops under the top bar so that it stays reachable), moved by dragging its header (pointer events), by tapping it, or with the "Réduire / Agrandir le panneau" buttons. The `title` slot is the only thing shown when collapsed, and the hidden content is `inert`. Below md the sheet is `overflow-hidden` (collapsed, its emptied content area keeps its padding and border, which spilled onto the tab bar, #142) and the tab bar sits above it (`z-20`); from md up the aside stays `overflow-visible` for its collapse tab.
- From md up the side panel collapses with the tab on its left edge ("Réduire le panneau", `aria-expanded`); the map then takes the whole width and the `title` slot floats at the top right, left of MapLibre's controls, beside "Afficher le panneau". Focus moves to the button that replaces the clicked one. `aside[data-panel]` = `expanded` / `collapsed`.
- Its left edge is a `role="separator"` ("Redimensionner le panneau", `aria-valuenow` = width in px): dragged with the pointer (pointer capture), or Left / Right arrows (16 px, ×4 with Shift), Home / End for the narrowest / widest panel. Width between 320 and 720 px, and at most the window minus 360 px for the map (`sidePanelWidth`, recomputed on window resize; the saved width is kept as dragged). The layout sets `--panel-width` on its root, used by the aside from md up.
- Collapsed state and width are kept in localStorage (`app/utils/sidePanel.ts`, key `sport-map:side-panel`), read on mount (the SSR page shows the panel open at 384 px), and ignored below md.
- When the map alone changes width (the side panel collapsed, expanded or resized), `MapView` keeps its left edge in place (`panBy` half the change) instead of its centre, so the map doesn't jump; window resizes keep MapLibre's default (centre kept). Framing needs nothing more: the map's own size already excludes the panel.
- `MapTopBar` floats at the top left of the map (MapLibre's controls are top right, scale and attribution at the bottom): back button when the page passes `back` (its fallback when there is no previous page in the app, read from `history.state.back`), logo, and `AccountMenu compact` (same menu as the header, button "Mon compte"). Its top margin uses `env(safe-area-inset-top)`. From md up, under the app header, it keeps only the back button (hidden without one); the editor keeps it whole. Discover, being a section, has no back button.
- The optional `search` slot (editor: `SearchBox places-only`) sits under the top bar on phones and tablets, between it and MapLibre's controls, and beside it from lg up. `useMapPadding` counts it in the top inset; the full sheet still stops under the bar alone. A picked place moves the map with `focusOn(map, focus)` from `useMapPadding` (bbox → `fitBounds`, else `flyTo`), never adding a waypoint.
- The map stops above the collapsed sheet (attribution and scale stay visible) and never resizes when the sheet moves. Views framed on a route or place use `useMapPadding()` (`padding(base)` for `fitBounds`, `offset()` for `flyTo`) so they stay under the top bar and above the open sheet. Never pass `padding` to `easeTo` / `flyTo`: MapLibre would keep it for every later move.
- The page may bind `v-model:snap` (the route page collapses the sheet during a flyover). The optional `overlay` slot (flyover controls) floats at the bottom of the map: above the sheet's edge on phones, following it through `--sheet-height` (set on the layout root; hidden when the sheet is full or dragged above half), centred above the scale and attribution from md up; the map container is `md:relative` for it.
- On the home page, the results line under the filters is `max-md:sr-only`: the sheet title shows it on phones, and the `aria-live` announcement stays. Likewise the `h1` of the route and collection pages (#100): the sheet title shows the name on phones, so it is shown only once.
- Header (#154): 56 px (`--ui-header-height: 3.5rem` in `main.css`; the layouts, the settings' sticky menu and the login page read the variable). On the map pages `MapSheetLayout` passes `wide`: the content spans the whole width like the map; text pages keep the centred column, aligned on their content. Its links and buttons keep their 32 px look, with an `::after` layer stretching their touch zone to 40 px (non-scoped style in `AppHeader.vue`: UHeader's slot content carries no scope id the selector could hang on).
- `UHeader` names its logo link after the `title` slot's text, HTML comments included in dev: keep comments out of that slot.
- e2e (`specs/layout.spec.ts`): one test measures the header (height, full width on the home page, column on the profile, touch zone); the phone test drives the sheet with buttons, the keyboard and a mouse drag, and checks the full sheet ends on the tab bar; another checks with `elementFromPoint` that nothing covers the tab bar under the collapsed sheet (it lifts `inert` during the check: hit testing skips inert elements, which would hide an overflow); the test account goes through the main navigation, creation, recording, the account menu and back buttons on a phone and a computer; other tests collapse, reopen (keyboard), resize (mouse, keyboard) and reload the side panel on a computer.
