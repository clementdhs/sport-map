# Main navigation, map pages: side panel and mobile bottom sheet

- Main navigation (#101, `useMainNav`): Carte (`/`, also current on route pages), Découvrir, Coach (`/coach`, `i-lucide-activity`; in place of "En direct" since #262), Profil (also current on collections), with `aria-current` (`page` on the section itself, `true` on its sub-pages). Below md, `AppTabBar` at the bottom (labels always shown, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) with a central "Enregistrer, partager ou créer" button opening a menu (`useNewItems`: Enregistrer une sortie, Partager ma position, Créer un parcours); from md up, `AppHeader` holds the same sections and the three buttons (styled by the item's `kind`, not its index; labels from lg, the share's from xl, which three labels need), on every page but the full-screen ones. "Partager ma position" opens `/record?share=1`, which asks to share once the outing has started (`live-sharing.md`); `/live` has no tab any more, reached from the home banner and `/live/:id`. Full screen, without header nor tab bar: editor (`MapSheetLayout fullscreen`), follow, record, a live share (`/live/:id`), which keep their own "Quitter" or back button. `AccountMenu` keeps only the account: settings, language, sign out.
- Home, discover, route page, editor and collection page use `MapSheetLayout` (slots `map`, `title`, `search`, `overlay`, default) and `definePageMeta({ layout: false })`. The layout is a `h-dvh` column: `AppHeader` (md up), its root (map, sheet, panel), `AppTabBar` (phones), so every measure taken on the root (sheet positions, `useMapPadding`) already leaves the bars out. Other pages use the default layout: `AppHeader` (below md only logo and account), and the tab bar fixed at the bottom with room kept under `UMain`. From md up the panel sits beside the map; below md the map fills the screen under a bottom sheet with three positions (`data-sheet` = `collapsed` / `half` / `full`, which stops under the top bar so that it stays reachable), moved by dragging its header (pointer events) or by tapping it (collapsed → half, open → collapsed); the header is also a `role="button"` (`aria-expanded`, named by the `title` slot) toggled the same way by Enter / Space, the only keyboard and screen reader access; it has no arrow buttons (removed in #247, not in the mockups, no replacement control). The `title` slot is the only thing shown when collapsed, and the hidden content is `inert`. Below md the sheet is `overflow-hidden` (collapsed, its emptied content area keeps its padding and border, which spilled onto the tab bar, #142) and the tab bar sits above it (`z-20`); from md up the aside stays `overflow-visible` for its collapse tab. Its scroll area is `relative` (#327): otherwise an absolute descendant without a positioned ancestor (an `sr-only` text, a hidden input) is placed against the aside, escapes the scroll area's clipping and, once below the fold, makes the whole page scroll past the window (a blank strip under the map on a computer).
- From md up the side panel collapses with the tab on its left edge ("Réduire le panneau", `aria-expanded`; a 20 × 56 px surface tab with a sand edge, kept over the map rather than inside the panel as in the mockup, so it covers neither the panel's content nor the inner half of the resize handle); the map then takes the whole width and the `title` slot floats at the top right, left of MapLibre's controls, beside "Afficher le panneau". Focus moves to the button that replaces the clicked one. `aside[data-panel]` = `expanded` / `collapsed`.
- Its left edge is a `role="separator"` ("Redimensionner le panneau", `aria-valuenow` = width in px): dragged with the pointer (pointer capture), or Left / Right arrows (16 px, ×4 with Shift), Home / End for the narrowest / widest panel. Width between 320 and 720 px, and at most the window minus 360 px for the map (`sidePanelWidth`, recomputed on window resize; the saved width is kept as dragged). The layout sets `--panel-width` on its root, used by the aside from md up.
- Collapsed state and width are kept in localStorage (`app/utils/sidePanel.ts`, key `sport-map:side-panel`), read on mount (the SSR page shows the panel open at 384 px), and ignored below md.
- When the map alone changes width (the side panel collapsed, expanded or resized), `MapView` keeps its left edge in place (`panBy` half the change) instead of its centre, so the map doesn't jump; window resizes keep MapLibre's default (centre kept). Framing needs nothing more: the map's own size already excludes the panel.
- `MapTopBar` floats at the top left of the map (MapLibre's controls are top right, scale and attribution at the bottom), a `map-float` bar (`main.css`: the surface, `bg-elevated` in dark mode, a sand ring and a soft shadow, like MapLibre's control groups; the collapsed panel's title uses it too): back button when the page passes `back` (its fallback when there is no previous page in the app, read from `history.state.back`), logo, and `AccountMenu compact` (same menu as the header, button "Mon compte"). Its top margin uses `env(safe-area-inset-top)`. From md up, under the app header, it keeps only the back button (hidden without one); the editor keeps it whole. Discover, being a section, has no back button.
- The optional `search` slot (editor: `SearchBox places-only`) sits under the top bar on phones and tablets, between it and MapLibre's controls, and beside it from lg up. `useMapPadding` counts it in the top inset; the full sheet still stops under the bar alone. A picked place moves the map with `focusOn(map, focus)` from `useMapPadding` (bbox → `fitBounds`, else `flyTo`), never adding a waypoint.
- The map stops above the collapsed sheet (attribution and scale stay visible) and never resizes when the sheet moves. Views framed on a route or place use `useMapPadding()` (`padding(base)` for `fitBounds`, `offset()` for `flyTo`) so they stay under the top bar and above the open sheet. Never pass `padding` to `easeTo` / `flyTo`: MapLibre would keep it for every later move.
- The page may bind `v-model:snap` (the route page collapses the sheet during a flyover). The optional `overlay` slot (flyover controls, the editor's "place the start" instruction) floats at the bottom of the map: above the sheet's edge on phones, following it through `--sheet-above-map` (the sheet's height above the map's bottom edge: the map stops above the collapsed sheet, so the half sheet's 50 % of the layout is `50% - peek / 2` of the map; hidden when the sheet is full or dragged above half), centred above the scale and attribution from md up; the map container is `md:relative` for it.
- Home sheet (#232): the title slot is a small-capitals muted label followed by a sand rule ("2 parcours ici"); from md up the same line (`aria-live`) is a Barlow Condensed heading. The sport filter is the `SportChips` row (`theme.md`), in place of the default sport's quick toggle. /discover opens on a contoured header (`ContourTexture`, `HerePlate`, `h1` shown on phones too), the OSM kinds as `SportChips`, and its results under a small-capitals heading, as cards on the surface.
- The sheet opens at half height, except on the home page (#197), which binds `v-model:snap` to open it collapsed: the map comes first, and the sheet title still gives the results count.
- On the home page, the results line under the filters is `max-md:sr-only`: the sheet title shows it on phones, and the `aria-live` announcement stays. Likewise the `h1` of the route and collection pages (#100): the sheet title shows the name on phones, so it is shown only once.
- Header (#154): 56 px (`--ui-header-height: 3.5rem` in `main.css`; the layouts, the settings' sticky menu and the login page read the variable). On the map pages `MapSheetLayout` passes `wide`: the content spans the whole width like the map; text pages keep the centred column, aligned on their content. Its links and buttons keep their 32 px look, with an `::after` layer stretching their touch zone to 40 px (non-scoped style in `AppHeader.vue`: UHeader's slot content carries no scope id the selector could hang on).
- `UHeader` names its logo link after the `title` slot's text, HTML comments included in dev: keep comments out of that slot.
- e2e (`specs/layout.spec.ts`): one test measures the header (height, full width on the home page, column on the profile, touch zone); the phone test drives the sheet with buttons, the keyboard and a mouse drag, and checks the full sheet ends on the tab bar; another checks with `elementFromPoint` that nothing covers the tab bar under the collapsed sheet (it lifts `inert` during the check: hit testing skips inert elements, which would hide an overflow); the test account goes through the main navigation (Coach, no En direct), creation, recording, sharing, the account menu and back buttons on a phone and a computer; other tests collapse, reopen (keyboard), resize (mouse, keyboard) and reload the side panel on a computer.

# Topo look of the navigation chrome (#231)

From the canvas's "PC — Carte" and "Final — Carte" boards (#226).

- `AppHeader`: solid surface with a sand bottom border (UHeader's default blur dropped), logo 30 px and "sportmap" in Barlow Condensed. Sections are neutral ghost buttons, 38 px high: the current one on a `bg-elevated` pill in bold ink, the others muted. "Enregistrer" is outlined in ink (`ring-(--ui-border-inverted)`, 1.5 px) with a red `bg-error` dot in place of an icon; "Créer" solid in the accent. `AccountMenu` shows the member's avatar (`MemberAvatar`: photo, else initials on the accent), 36 px in the header, 32 px in `MapTopBar`; the account name is in its aria-label and at the top of the menu.
- `AppTabBar`: surface, sand top border, current tab in the accent and bold; the central button a 52 px rounded square (18 px radius) in the accent with a dark shadow; Profil shows the member's photo once chosen, its icon before (`avatars.md`).
- `MapSheetLayout`: the phone sheet and the desktop panel are on the paper (`bg-paper`), the sheet with 22 px top corners, an upward shadow and a `--ui-border-accented` handle; the panel with a sand (`border-accented`) left edge, no shadow.
- MapLibre's buttons are 40 px, 12 px from the edges and 8 px apart (`main.css`): the first margin is the container's `padding-top`, since the hidden zoom group on phones would take a `:first-child` margin with it. Not the mockup's 44 px on phones: the top bars of follow mode, recording and live shares keep 56 px for them (`pr-14`, `readability.md`). The top area stops `right-15` short of them (the collapsed panel's title `right-16`), and the full sheet's `--sheet-top` counts the 44 px `MapTopBar` (`+ 3.5rem`).
